INVESTMENT OBJECTIVE AND STRATEGY
The Gryphon Capital Income Trust (ASX: GCI) aims to provide investors with a reliable, sustainable 
monthly income, targeting a return of RBA Cash Rate + 3.50% p.a. GCI invests primarily in the 
Australian securitisation market, focusing on both public market securitised bonds and private 
warehouse facilities. The GCI investment portfolio is predominantly comprised of Residential 
Mortgage‑Backed Securities (RMBS) and Asset‑Backed Securities (ABS).

COMMENTARY
MARKET ACTIVITY 
February was another exceptionally strong month for Australian securitisation markets, with more 
than A$12bn of primary RMBS and ABS issuance across one ABS and eight RMBS transactions. 
This included a standout A$2.7bn non‑bank deala, now the largest of its kind ever issued in Australia.
This momentum follows a very busy 2025, where overall issuance finished just shy of a 
record‑breaking year, underscoring the depth and resilience of the market despite reduced 
ADI issuance. 
Perhaps most importantly for investors, the market has continued to absorb substantial supply without 
any signs of fatigue. Transactions have been met with healthy demand, allowing issuers to clear deals 
smoothly and maintain tight pricing. 
CAPITAL MANAGEMENT 
In February, and in line with its investment strategy as outlined in the Product Disclosure Statement 
dated 29 January 2024, GCI successfully raised approximately A$90.9 million through a wholesale 
placement of 45.45 million new unitsb. This additional capital supports ongoing portfolio management 
and positions GCI to capitalise on a growing pipeline of attractive opportunities.
A larger GCI vehicle aims to deliver tangible benefits for unitholders:
•	 Broader Portfolio Diversity:
Increased scale enhances GCI’s ability to participate across both public and private RMBS/ABS 
markets, improving diversification and supporting consistent income generation.
•	 Greater ASX Liquidity:
Prior to the placement, GCI already had more than 11,900 unitholders. The expansion of the 
register through the current Offer is expected to further improve secondary market liquidity for 
all investors.
•	 Lower Operating Costs per Unit:
As GCI grows, fixed operating costs are spread across a larger base, reducing the cost per unit and 
improving overall efficiency.
Gryphon undertook this capital raise to ensure GCI could continue to execute on a significant pipeline 
of opportunities that meet GCI’s risk return objectives. Many of these opportunities have been under 
development since Q4 2025, and we look forward to updating unitholders as proceeds are deployed 
over the coming months.

ASSET SECTORS
Prime RMBS
Secured by a diversified pool of first-ranking mortgage loans to prime 
borrowers backed by residential properties. 
Non-Conforming RMBS
Secured by a diversified pool of first-ranking mortgage loans backed by 
residential properties made to borrowers that would not typically qualify 
for a Prime loan. Borrowers may not qualify as they are self-employed, 
seeking a large loan size, don’t have required proof of income, or have a 
few dents in their credit records.
ABS SME
Secured by a diversified pool of first-ranking mortgage loans to self-
managed superannuation, companies or individual borrowers, backed by 
small ticket commercial or residential properties.    
ABS Auto
Secured by a diversified pool of first-ranking Australian automotive lease 
and loan receivables.
ABS Consumer
Secured by a diversified pool of unsecured and secured personal loans 
extended to borrowers located in Australia.
