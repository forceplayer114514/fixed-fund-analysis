INVESTMENT OBJECTIVE AND STRATEGY
The Gryphon Capital Income Trust (ASX: GCI) aims to provide investors with a reliable, sustainable 
monthly income, targeting a return of RBA Cash Rate + 3.50% p.a. GCI invests primarily in the 
Australian securitisation market, focusing on both public market securitised bonds and private 
warehouse facilities. The GCI investment portfolio is predominantly comprised of Residential 
Mortgage‑Backed Securities (RMBS) and Asset‑Backed Securities (ABS).

COMMENTARY
MARKET TEMPO SLOWS, BUT PRIMARY PIPELINE BUILDS
Following a strong year for securitised markets, it is not uncommon for activity to pause over the 
holiday period. Consistent with this seasonal pattern, public primary issuance has remained subdued 
through January.
While the informal convention for these public transactions to reach investors ahead of Australia Day 
has now passed, we continue to hear that a sizeable first-quarter pipeline is building across both RMBS 
and ABS markets. As issuers move beyond the holiday window, execution now becomes critical.
ORIGINATION ADVANTAGE SUPPORTS DISCIPLINED DEPLOYMENT
Gryphon maintains a robust, repeatable investment process which seeks to ensure consistency in 
portfolio construction through varying market conditions. This discipline has supported GCI’s ability 
to deliver sustainable monthly income whilst maintaining a strong focus on capital preservation.
Importantly, Gryphon is active across public term markets, private term placements and warehouse 
securitisations. These opportunities can offer attractive structuring and pricing dynamics relative to 
broadly syndicated deals, supporting selective deployment and portfolio optimisation.
Irrespective of the market tempo, our approach remains disciplined, asset-led and focused on 
delivering sustainable income with strong capital preservation.

ASSET SECTORS
Prime RMBS
Secured by a diversified pool of first-ranking mortgage loans to prime 
borrowers backed by residential properties. 
Non-Conforming RMBS
Secured by a diversified pool of first-ranking mortgage loans backed by 
residential properties made to borrowers that would not typically qualify 
for a Prime loan. Borrowers may not qualify as they are self-employed, 
seeking a large loan size, don’t have required proof of income, or have a 
few dents in their credit records.
ABS SME
Secured by a diversified pool of first-ranking mortgage loans to self-
managed superannuation, companies or individual borrowers, backed by 
small ticket commercial or residential properties.    
ABS Auto
Secured by a diversified pool of first-ranking Australian automotive lease 
and loan receivables.
ABS Consumer
Secured by a diversified pool of unsecured and secured personal loans 
extended to borrowers located in Australia.
