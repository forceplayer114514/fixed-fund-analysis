INVESTMENT OBJECTIVE AND STRATEGY
The Gryphon Capital Income Trust (ASX Code: GCI) is a listed trust designed to provide investors 
with sustainable, monthly income (Target Return equal to RBA Cash Rate +3.50% pa) through 
exposure to the Australian Securitisation market. This asset-class, primarily consisting of Residential 
Mortgage‑Backed Securities (RMBS) and Asset Backed Securities (ABS), is a key pillar of the Australian 
fixed income market.

COMMENTARY 
MARKET ACTIVITY 
In October, the Australian RMBS/ABS new issue market saw thirteen transactions priced, totalling 
approximately A$10.85 billion. This included five public asset-backed securities (ABS) across the 
Auto, Personal Loans, BNPL, and Small-Ticket Commercial sectors. Since the end of August, more 
than A$20 billion of RMBS/ABS has been priced. While transactions continue to get done, softer 
coverage levels and investor reluctance to push spreads tighter indicate a more selective and 
discerning market environment.
CPI AND MONETARY POLICY UPDATE
The RBA’s November Statement on Monetary Policy highlighted that, although headline inflation has 
moderated, underlying inflation remains elevated. Risks are skewed towards inflation staying higher 
for longer. As a result, the Monetary Policy Board judged it appropriate to leave the cash rate target 
unchanged at 3.60% and remains cautious about the outlook.
CAPITAL MANAGEMENT 
In October, Gryphon offered all eligible unitholders the opportunity to participate in the Unit Purchase 
Plan (UPP), allowing applications for up to A$30,000 in new units at an offer price of A$2.00 per unit 
— matching the price paid by wholesale investors in the recent placement announced to the ASX on 
28 August 20251.
The UPP was very well supported, with unitholders applying for approximately A$57 million in new 
units. This strong demand has lifted GCI’s market capitalisation to around A$1.2 billion2.
