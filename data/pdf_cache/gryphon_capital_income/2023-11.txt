Investment Objective and Strategy
The Gryphon Capital Income Trust (ASX Code: GCI) is a listed trust designed to provide investors with 
sustainable, monthly income (Target Return equal to RBA Cash Rate + 3.50% pa) through exposure to the 
Australian Securitisation market. This asset-class, primarily consisting of Residential Mortgage-Backed 
Securities (RMBS) and Asset Backed Securities (ABS), is a key pillar of the Australian fixed income market.

Commentary
Prior to markets starting to wind down for the Christmas holidays, we witnessed a robust primary 
new-issue calendar in November with $6.1 billion issued via eight transactions. There was a total of six 
RMBS transactions issued in the month (including three Prime RMBS and three NC RMBS) and two ABS 
transactions.
The Gryphon Investment Committee has continued its stance since October, of refraining from 
participating in any of the new-issue transactions, due to a combination of credit, relative value, and 
portfolio optimisation considerations. Away from the new-issue market, we have identified and are 
pursuing a number of compelling investment opportunities. However, due to the irregular nature of the 
market’s liquidity, this requires the running of higher cash balances in the short term. It is expected that 
these investment opportunities will be funded by the end of the year.
