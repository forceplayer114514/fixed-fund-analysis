INVESTMENT OBJECTIVE AND STRATEGY
The Gryphon Capital Income Trust (ASX Code: GCI) is a listed trust designed to provide investors 
with sustainable, monthly income (Target Return equal to RBA Cash Rate +3.50% pa) through 
exposure to the Australian Securitisation market. This asset-class, primarily consisting of Residential 
Mortgage‑Backed Securities (RMBS) and Asset Backed Securities (ABS), is a key pillar of the Australian 
fixed income market.

COMMENTARY 
August saw robust activity in the Australian securitisation market, with five public new issues — 
four RMBS and one Auto & Equipment ABS — attracting strong demand from both domestic and 
international investors. High subscription ratios across the capital structure reflect continued 
confidence in the sector.
MARKET DRIVERS & ECONOMIC BACKDROP
The appeal of Australian securitised products to offshore investors is underpinned not only by 
compelling relative value but also by the strength of the Australian consumer and the resilience of 
underlying asset classes. The Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) cut rates by 25bps in August, bringing 
the cash rate to 3.60% — a cumulative 75bps reduction since February. These cuts have offered some 
relief to households still grappling with elevated inflation and mortgage costs.
While July’s inflation data exceeded expectations, the labour market remains stable and robust. 
This is a positive signal for borrower repayment capacity, with home loan arrears remaining 
surprisingly benign. Prime RMBS arrears have held steady over the past year, and non-conforming 
RMBS arrears have actually improved1.
However, borrowers who originated loans in 2021–2022 — prior to the RBA’s tightening cycle — 
are experiencing more acute cost-of-living pressures. Arrears in this cohort continue to edge higher 
and remain a key focus for Gryphon’s surveillance of higher-risk exposures.
In the Auto ABS space, 30+ day arrears are largely unchanged year-on-year2, supported by strong 
employment data. More seriously however, 60+ arrears are gradually widening2 as cost of living 
pressures have been hurting those Auto borrowers who are already behind in their payments. 
Unlike mortgages, which are typically floating rate, auto loans are fixed rate, meaning recent rate 
cuts offer no direct relief.
CAPITAL MANAGEMENT
During August, to support ongoing portfolio management aligned with GCI’s investment strategy 
(as outlined in the Product Disclosure Statement dated 29 January 2024), GCI raised A$75 million 
via a wholesale placement of 37.5 million new units.
Additionally, the Responsible Entity has announced its intention to launch a Unit Purchase Plan 
(UPP) for existing unitholders. Eligible investors will be able to apply for up to A$30,000 in new units, 
offering an equitable opportunity to increase holdings. Full offer documentation and the timetable 
will be released shortly.
